# Our Development Process - TIA Software Solutions

We follow a high-fidelity, iterative engineering methodology to guarantee success:

## Phase 1: Discovery & Scoping (1-2 days)
Collect business objectives, target audience requirements, feature checklists, and target timelines to draft a formal quotation.

## Phase 2: Design & Prototyping (1-2 weeks)
Create modern, interactive UI layout designs in Figma. We review wireframes and high-fidelity mockups with you to align on design direction.

## Phase 3: Engineering & Optimization (2-4 weeks)
Develop components using clean React, TailwindCSS, TypeScript, and fast static builds. We implement SEO tags, responsive CSS, and micro-animations.

## Phase 4: Quality & Testing (3-5 days)
Cross-browser compatibility checks, Lighthouse performance audits, mobile responsiveness testing, and pixel tracking validations.

## Phase 5: Launch & Handover (1-2 days)
Deploy the build to netlify/Vercel, link custom domains, set up analytics tools, and hand over CMS documentation for content updates.
