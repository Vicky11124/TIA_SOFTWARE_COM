# Industry
The construction and trade sector includes general contractors, commercial builders, home renovation specialists, roofers, civil engineering firms, architects, and sub-contractors (electrical, plumbing, HVAC). Construction clients evaluate companies based on portfolio craftsmanship, project scale, safety accreditations, client testimonials, and transparent estimation capabilities.

# Business Goals
- Win high-value residential remodeling, extension, and commercial building contracts
- Establish trust with homeowners, property developers, and commercial architects
- Showcase high-quality project portfolios with clear before-and-after transformations
- Generate incoming project estimate and RFP (Request for Proposal) inquiries
- Highlight safety accreditations, trade licenses, insurance coverage, and compliance standards
- Streamline client consultation booking and site survey requests
- Recruit skilled tradespeople and sub-contractors via dedicated careers pages

# Common Digital Problems
- Basic, outdated websites featuring low-quality smartphone photos of job sites
- Lack of detailed project case studies showing scope, timeline, and architectural details
- Absence of online quote request forms or project budget estimation tools
- Low local search ranking for lucrative regional terms ("home extension builder [city]", "commercial contractor")
- Missing trade accreditations (e.g. Checkatrade, FMB, CHAS, ISO, OSHA, Master Builders)
- Slow website loading due to uncompressed project photo galleries
- Weak mobile accessibility making phone inquiries from job sites difficult

# Consultant Responses
1. **Word-of-Mouth Dependency**: "Word-of-mouth gets you invited to tender, but when a homeowner or developer evaluates a £100,000+ contract, they inspect your website to verify your craftsmanship, safety accreditations, and client reviews. A weak website loses high-value jobs to competitors."
2. **High-Value Lead Behavior**: "Modern property developers and commercial clients do perform digital due diligence. A professional TIA website with detailed case studies, drone footage, and trade accreditations elevates your brand from 'local builder' to 'trusted principal contractor'."
3. **Full Order Book**: "When your order book is full, that's the precise time to upgrade your web presence. It gives you the leverage to raise your profit margins and select higher-yield luxury builds rather than competing on price for basic jobs."
4. **Online Quote Form Concerns**: "Online quote tools do not give binding estimates—they act as interactive lead qualifiers. They gather key details (project type, floor area, budget range, blueprint PDFs) so you can filter out tire-kickers and focus your estimators' time on high-margin leads."
5. **High Prices**: "A single additional extension or commercial refurbishment contract generates £20,000 to £100,000+ in revenue. A TIA platform that wins even *one extra contract per year* delivers an ROI of over 1,000%."

# Industry Best Practices
- **Visual Proof First**: Use high-definition before/after sliders and drone videos in the hero section to demonstrate tangible craftsmanship instantly.
- **Accreditation Dominance**: Display safety, trade, and insurance badges prominently beneath the main navigation bar to remove buyer anxiety.
- **Detailed Case Studies**: Include breakdown details (architect, floor area, challenge, structural solution, timeline, final result) for every flagship project.
