# Industry
The fitness, gym, and wellness sector includes commercial gyms, boutique fitness studios, personal training studios, yoga & pilates centers, CrossFit boxes, martial arts academies, and online fitness coaches. Fitness businesses thrive on recurring monthly memberships, free trial pass signups, class bookings, personal training packages, and inspiring transformation proof.

# Business Goals
- Drive recurring monthly gym membership subscriptions and personal training packages
- Increase free trial pass requests and guest day-pass conversions
- Streamline group class timetable browsing and online spot reservations
- Showcase client body transformation results, coach credentials, and studio facilities
- Rank on Page 1 of local search for high-intent queries ("gym near me", "pilates studio [city]")
- Automate member onboarding, liability waivers, and monthly billing
- Sell supplementary fitness supplements, merchandise, and digital workout plans

# Common Digital Problems
- Dated, uninspiring websites with clunky mobile timetables that fail to render correctly on phones
- Lack of online membership checkout, requiring prospective members to visit in person during sales hours
- Complex class booking systems that confuse members or crash during peak reservation drops
- Absence of real transformation stories, before/after photos, or video member testimonials
- Low search visibility for specific fitness niches (e.g., "Reformer Pilates [Area]", "HYROX training")
- High member churn due to lack of post-signup engagement and automated communication
- Non-functional lead capture forms that miss prospective member inquiries

# Consultant Responses
1. **App vs Web Discovery**: "Gym apps are great for existing members, but prospective members looking for a new gym start on Google, not in app stores. A custom TIA website ranks on Google, showcases your culture, and turns casual scrollers into paying monthly members."
2. **Transparent Pricing Advantage**: "Hiding membership prices creates friction and suspicion. 75% of prospective gym members abandon sites that obscure prices. Clear pricing paired with a 'Free Trial Pass' lead magnet builds trust and converts leads faster than secretive competitors."
3. **Wix vs Custom Fitness Engine**: "Generic web templates have clunky timetable widgets that break on mobile screens. TIA builds custom, sub-second web platforms engineered specifically for seamless mobile timetable browsing and 1-click membership signups."
4. **Free Trial Lead Nurturing**: "Free trial leads only fail when there is no automated follow-up. Pairing your free trial web form with automated TIA SMS & email drip sequences increases trial-to-paid membership conversion by up to 35%."
5. **High Prices**: "An average gym member stays for 12 months, generating £400 to £1,200+ in lifetime value. Acquiring just 3 extra monthly members through your new web platform completely covers the monthly investment."

# Industry Best Practices
- **Motivation & Proof First**: Feature high-energy video clips, facility highlights, and real member transformation stories above the fold.
- **Low-Friction Lead Capture**: Offer a prominent "Claim Free 7-Day Pass" button on every page to capture prospect contact details.
- **Sub-Second Timetable Loading**: Ensure the mobile class schedule loads instantly without annoying popups or horizontal scrolling.
