# Industry
The e-commerce and digital retail sector includes direct-to-consumer (D2C) brands, fashion boutiques, consumer electronics retailers, niche specialty stores, B2B wholesale portals, and subscription box services. Online stores require high conversion rates, rapid load speeds, seamless multi-currency checkout, robust inventory synchronization, and data-driven customer retention loops.

# Business Goals
- Increase online store conversion rates (CVR) and average order value (AOV)
- Reduce shopping cart abandonment rates across desktop and mobile
- Provide frictionless sub-second page loads for large product catalogs
- Integrate multi-currency payment gateways and Buy-Now-Pay-Later (BNPL) options (Klarna, Clearpay, Afterpay)
- Automate inventory synchronization between online store and warehouse/ERP systems
- Drive repeat purchases through automated post-purchase email & SMS marketing campaigns
- Scale international sales with localized tax, currency, and shipping rules

# Common Digital Problems
- Slow website load speeds caused by unoptimized product images and bloated plugins
- High mobile cart abandonment due to complex, multi-step checkout processes
- Poor mobile UX, broken search filters, and inadequate product recommendation engines
- Lack of trust signals (customer reviews, security badges, clear refund policies)
- Disconnected inventory management causing overselling of out-of-stock items
- Absence of post-purchase automated email sequences (abandoned cart, cross-sell, review requests)
- Low organic search rankings for competitive product keywords and collection pages

# Industry Best Practices
- **Sub-Second Speed**: Compress all product images to WebP, use lazy loading, and host assets on a global CDN to ensure mobile PDPs load under 1.5 seconds.
- **Frictionless Mobile Checkout**: Implement 1-click express checkout options (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay) prominently above the fold on mobile carts.
- **Social Proof & Urgency**: Display real-time customer reviews, photo ratings, low-stock indicators, and free shipping threshold progress bars.
