# Industry
The automotive sector includes car dealerships (used and new), auto repair & mechanic garages, vehicle detailing centers, car rental agencies, auto parts suppliers, and specialized tuning shops. Automotive businesses rely on vehicle inventory showcases, test drive bookings, service appointment scheduling, financing calculators, and trust-building customer reviews.

# Business Goals
- Win vehicle sales instructions and generate qualified test drive leads
- Drive online booking for garage MOTs, vehicle servicing, and detailing appointments
- Showcase vehicle inventory with high-resolution 360-degree photo galleries and specs
- Provide finance calculation tools (PCP, HP, Leasing) and online finance application forms
- Rank at the top of local search engines for automotive queries ("used cars in [City]", "mechanic near me")
- Streamline trade-in vehicle valuation inquiries to acquire quality used car stock
- Build customer trust through verified reviews, MOT reminders, and transparent repair quotes

# Common Digital Problems
- Slow, clunky vehicle inventory search engines that crash on mobile devices
- Dependency on third-party auto portals (AutoTrader, CarGurus, Cars.com) charging high listing fees
- Lack of online service & MOT booking calendars, requiring phone calls during garage hours
- Outdated car listing pages missing key vehicle specs, service history, or finance details
- Absence of instant trade-in valuation forms, missing out on car acquiring opportunities
- Poor local SEO making garage services invisible against large national auto repair chains
- Non-responsive finance calculators that confuse prospective car buyers
