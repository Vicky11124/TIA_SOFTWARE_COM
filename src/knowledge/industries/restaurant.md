# Industry
The food and hospitality sector includes independent restaurants, cafes, fine dining establishments, bakeries, food trucks, bistros, bars, and ghost kitchens. Businesses rely heavily on online visibility, high-impact food visuals, frictionless menu browsing, table reservations, and direct online ordering to maximize cover counts and margin retention.

# Business Goals
- Increase table reservations during weekday and weekend peak times
- Drive commission-free direct online delivery and takeaway orders
- Showcase food, ambiance, and chef specials through high-resolution media
- Build customer loyalty and repeat visits through email/SMS marketing
- Rank on Page 1 of Google Maps for local searches ("best restaurant near me")
- Streamline private event, catering, and function inquiries
- Reduce reliance on third-party aggregator apps (UberEats, Deliveroo, DoorDash) that charge 25–35% commissions

# Common Digital Problems
- Outdated static websites with unreadable PDF menus on mobile devices
- Excessive commissions paid to third-party delivery platforms cutting into thin margins
- Lack of direct online table booking, leading to missed phone inquiries during dinner rush
- Poor local SEO and missing or unverified Google Business Profile listings
- Slow page load times with unoptimized food photography causing high bounce rates
- No integration between website bookings and point-of-sale (POS) systems
- Weak visual branding and absence of customer reviews or social proof

# Consultant Responses
1. **UberEats Dependency**: "Third-party delivery apps are great for discovery, but paying 30% commissions on repeat customers drains your profits. A commission-free ordering portal on your site pays for itself after just 15–20 direct orders a month while keeping customer data in your hands."
2. **Instagram Only**: "Instagram is fantastic for visuals, but guests looking for opening hours, dietary options, or table availability on Google won't scroll through Instagram posts. A fast website converts passive scrollers into confirmed dinner reservations."
3. **Phone Reservations**: "During the evening dinner rush, your front-of-house staff should focus on dining guests rather than answering phone calls for availability. Online booking captures reservations 24/7, even while your kitchen is closed."
4. **Wix vs Custom Solution**: "Wix templates look fine initially, but unoptimized image galleries slow mobile load speeds, leading to dropped visits. TIA builds lightning-fast custom sites engineered to rank locally on Google and convert visitors in under 3 clicks."
5. **High Prices**: "Think of your website as your highest-performing waiter. It presents your menu, takes table bookings, sells gift vouchers, and captures leads 24/7 without taking days off. One extra group booking per week covers the entire monthly investment."

# Industry Best Practices
- **Mobile-First Experience**: 85% of restaurant searches occur on smartphones. Menus must load as HTML text (never PDF), with tap-to-call and tap-to-book buttons sticky on the screen.
- **Visual Storytelling**: High-quality lighting on hero dishes increases menu item order rate by up to 30%.
- **Zero-Friction Conversion**: Place the "Book a Table" and "Order Online" CTAs in the top navigation bar and hero header.
- **Trust Signals**: Display hygiene ratings, Google review scores (4.5+ stars), chef accolades, and customer testimonials prominently.
