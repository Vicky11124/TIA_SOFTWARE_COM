# Industry
The consulting and professional advisory sector includes management consultants, business coaches, strategy firms, HR & leadership consultancies, IT advisory practices, marketing agencies, and executive mentors. Consultants rely on thought leadership, executive credibility, quantifiable client case studies, high-value discovery call bookings, and proprietary methodology frameworks.

# Business Goals
- Win high-yield corporate advisory retainers and project consulting engagements
- Position senior consultants and founders as recognized industry thought leaders
- Showcase quantifiable client ROI results, methodology frameworks, and enterprise case studies
- Drive prospective client Discovery Call bookings with automated pre-call qualification questionnaires
- Capture enterprise leads through downloadable whitepapers, benchmark reports, and executive guides
- Build brand authority through client logo showcases, keynote speech reels, and media press features
- Rank on Page 1 of organic search for specialized consulting niches ("B2B sales strategy consultant", "HR restructuring advisory")

# Common Digital Problems
- Abstract, generic marketing copy ("We deliver synergistic enterprise transformation") that fails to articulate tangible business outcomes
- Outdated, text-heavy websites that look like traditional corporate brochures rather than modern digital engines
- High drop-off rate on contact pages due to lack of direct calendar booking tools or discovery call schedulers
- Absence of structured enterprise case studies showing clear before-and-after ROI metrics
- Poor mobile optimization making executive browsing frustrating on smartphones between meetings
- Lack of gated thought-leadership assets (whitepapers, benchmark reports) to capture early-stage enterprise leads
- Low organic search visibility for niche corporate consulting keywords

# Consultant Responses
1. **Network Validation & Due Diligence**: "Personal networks get you in the door, but before a board or CEO approves a £50,000+ consulting engagement, they inspect your website to verify your methodology, case studies, and corporate credentials. A weak website undermines your executive authority."
2. **Executive Lead Behavior**: "CEOs and CFOs consume digital content constantly. According to Gartner, 70% of the B2B buying journey happens digitally before contacting a sales representative. Gated benchmark reports and case studies attract executives during their strategic planning phase."
3. **Pre-Qualifying Discovery Calls**: "Interactive booking forms don't alienate executives—they respect their time. By asking 3 targeted pre-qualification questions (company revenue, timeline, key challenge), your senior partners only spend time on calls with qualified enterprise leads."
4. **Wix/Squarespace Limitations**: "Generic templates look like small business pages, failing to convey the gravitas required for high-stakes corporate advisory work. TIA delivers an executive-grade web platform engineered for thought leadership, CRM integration, and enterprise trust."
5. **High Prices**: "A single consulting retainer or strategy project generates £15,000 to £100,000+ in fees. A TIA platform that attracts just *one extra corporate engagement per year* yields an extraordinary return on investment."

# Industry Best Practices
- **Executive Gravity & Proof**: Display Fortune 500 client logos, quantifiable ROI metrics, and founder authority prominently above the fold.
- **2-Step Qualified Booking**: Combine a low-friction "Download Benchmark Report" lead magnet with an interactive "Book Executive Discovery Call" scheduler.
- **ROI-Driven Case Studies**: Structure every case study with 4 clear components: Enterprise Profile, Strategic Challenge, TIA Consulting Solution, and Measured ROI Results.
