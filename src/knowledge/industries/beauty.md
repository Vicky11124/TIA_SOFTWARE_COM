# Industry
The beauty, salon, and aesthetics sector includes hair salons, day spas, cosmetic clinics, lash/nail bars, skincare studios, tanning salons, and wellness retreats. Clients select beauty providers based on visual aesthetic proof, glowing client reviews, clear service price menus, seamless online booking, and practitioner expertise.

# Business Goals
- Fill salon appointment books across off-peak and peak time slots
- Drive online booking 24/7 without burdening front-desk staff during client appointments
- Sell digital gift vouchers and treatment packages online for birthdays, holidays, and events
- Build visual authority through high-end treatment portfolios, before-and-after cases, and interior aesthetic photos
- Rank on Page 1 of local search for high-demand beauty searches ("hair salon near me", "botox clinic [city]")
- Capture client deposit payments online to eliminate appointment no-shows
- Drive automated client re-booking and review requests via SMS and email marketing

# Common Digital Problems
- Outdated, uninspired websites that do not reflect the premium aesthetic of the physical salon
- Lack of real-time online appointment scheduling integrated with salon software
- High rate of last-minute appointment cancellations and uncollected no-show fees
- Unclear pricing, missing treatment duration details, or unreadable photo menus
- Weak local SEO causing loss of new client bookings to nearby competitor salons
- Absence of online gift voucher sales, missing out on seasonal holiday revenue
- Disconnected social media presence where Instagram followers cannot easily book treatments

# Consultant Responses
1. **Fresha/Instagram Limitations**: "Instagram and Fresha are great for scrollers, but third-party platforms control your client database and show competitor salons right next to your profile. A custom website builds your brand authority, captures organic Google searches, and retains 100% of your client relationship."
2. **Online Deposit Value**: "Requiring a nominal £10–£20 booking deposit online doesn't turn away good clients—it eliminates tire-kickers and reduces no-shows by up to 90%. Serious clients appreciate knowing their appointment slot is guaranteed."
3. **Wix vs Custom Aesthetic Site**: "A aesthetic clinic or luxury salon selling £200+ treatments cannot afford to look low-budget online. A custom TIA site delivers sub-second mobile speeds, high-end visual design, and SEO structure that positions you as the premier salon in your area."
4. **Automated Re-Booking ROI**: "An automated website system that sends a gentle SMS re-booking prompt 4 weeks post-treatment increases annual client visit frequency from 4 times to 6 times a year. That represents a 50% increase in revenue from your existing client base."
5. **High Prices**: "A single loyal salon client brings in £500 to £2,000+ per year in recurring visits, gift vouchers, and product purchases. Acquiring just 2 extra regular clients per month through your new web platform completely covers the monthly investment."

# Industry Best Practices
- **Visual Glamour & Speed**: Use high-definition, color-calibrated treatment imagery compressed for instant sub-second mobile load times.
- **2-Tap Booking Path**: Place a sticky "Book Treatment" button at the top and bottom of every mobile page.
- **Social Proof & Deposits**: Combine before/after visual sliders, 5-star Google review badges, and clear deposit policies to maximize booking conversions.
