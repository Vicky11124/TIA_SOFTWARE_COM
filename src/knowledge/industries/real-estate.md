# Industry
The real estate and property sector includes residential estate agencies, commercial brokerages, lettings management firms, property developers, and buyer's agents. Real estate businesses thrive on property seller valuations, landlord instruction leads, high-impact property listing showcases, virtual tours, and hyper-local community authority.

# Business Goals
- Win property seller appraisal and valuation instructions over local competitors
- Generate qualified landlord letting leads for property management services
- Showcase property listings with high-definition image galleries and floor plans
- Capture buyer and tenant inquiry leads instantly 24/7
- Establish hyper-local brand authority across specific neighborhoods and postcodes
- Reduce manual listing entry by syncing site with property portals (Rightmove, Zoopla, Zillow, Domain)
- Collect registered buyer preferences for automated property alerts

# Common Digital Problems
- Heavy dependency on third-party property portals (Rightmove, Zoopla, Zillow) that raise listing fees annually
- Outdated websites with sluggish property search filters and map views
- Slow loading of high-resolution property imagery causing user drop-off
- Absence of instant online home valuation calculators to capture vendor leads
- Poor mobile layout making property browsing and viewing requests frustrating
- Lack of video tours, 3D walkthroughs, or interactive floor plans
- Inability to showcase sold portfolio stats ("100+ Properties Sold in [Neighborhood]")

# Consultant Responses
1. **Rightmove/Portal Dependency**: "Property portals are essential, but their listing fees increase every year and they own your customer data. A custom website with an instant valuation engine captures vendor leads *before* they list on portals, saving thousands in commission and portal fees."
2. **Shopfront vs Website**: "High street presence builds local awareness, but 90% of home sellers research 3 local estate agents online before booking a valuation. A premium, fast website positions your agency as the modern, market-leading choice."
3. **CRM Template vs Custom Platform**: "Template sites provided by CRM vendors look identical to every competitor in your postcode. A custom TIA platform delivers unique branding, faster search performance, instant valuation leads, and superior Google rankings."
4. **Accuracy of Instant Valuation**: "The goal of an instant online valuation is not to deliver a final survey figure—it's a lead magnet designed to capture the seller's name, email, phone, and home address so your agents can follow up with a precise in-person appraisal."
5. **High Prices**: "Winning just *one extra home instruction* per month yields £3,000 to £8,000 in seller commission. A TIA platform that generates 5–10 new valuation inquiries monthly offers an astronomical ROI."

# Industry Best Practices
- **Lead-First Architecture**: Place the "Instant Home Valuation" search bar prominently in the hero section to turn passive scrollers into seller leads.
- **Visual Impact**: Use high-resolution webp image compression, responsive gallery sliders, and embedded Matterport 3D tours to keep buyers on-page longer.
- **Hyper-Local Authority**: Build dedicated landing pages for every postcode/neighborhood, featuring market stats, sold prices, and local guides.
