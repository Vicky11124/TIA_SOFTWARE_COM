# Industry
The retail and physical shop sector includes independent clothing boutiques, gift shops, furniture showrooms, specialty food stores, jewelry retailers, electronics shops, and multi-branch retail chains. Physical retailers rely on local foot-traffic generation, click-and-collect ordering, online store catalog browsing, store location finders, and stock availability checks.

# Business Goals
- Drive local foot traffic into physical retail stores and showrooms
- Offer Click-and-Collect (Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store - BOPIS) capabilities
- Showcase current in-store inventory and product collections through a digital shop portal
- Rank on Page 1 of local search engines for retail searches ("clothing boutique near me", "furniture shop [city]")
- Sell products directly online with local delivery and nation-wide shipping options
- Build customer loyalty through digital rewards programs, newsletter perks, and VIP event invites
- Capture customer reviews and highlight shop interior ambiance and staff expertise

# Common Digital Problems
- Total reliance on physical foot traffic, leaving the shop vulnerable during low-footfall seasons or poor weather
- Outdated websites that do not reflect current in-store stock levels or seasonal collections
- Absence of Click-and-Collect options, losing local shoppers who want immediate pickup
- Poor local SEO and missing Google Business Profile location mapping
- Slow loading product catalog images on mobile phones used by shoppers on high streets
- Disconnected Point of Sale (POS) system causing stock discrepancies between store and website
- Lack of digital gift voucher purchasing, missing out on local gift buyers
