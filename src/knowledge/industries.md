# Industry Nuances & Suggestions - TIA Software Solutions

We optimize project proposals with industry-intelligent recommendations:

## 1. Restaurants & Hospitality
- **Key Features**: Online Menu, Table Reservations booking widget, Google Maps location, and WhatsApp Direct Ordering.
- **Consultant Tip**: Suggest a high-speed mobile-first menu instead of heavy PDF links to improve customer retention.

## 2. Real Estate Agencies
- **Key Features**: Property Listings search, interactive map integration, CRM lead capture forms, and high-res property photo galleries.
- **Consultant Tip**: Recommend a fast headless CMS integration so real estate agents can add/update listings on the go.

## 3. Medical & Dental Clinics
- **Key Features**: Secure appointment scheduler, detailed doctor profiles, online consultation booking, and emergency clinic contacts.
- **Consultant Tip**: Highlight ease-of-use for elderly patients and clean, reassuring UI colors.

## 4. Gyms & Fitness Centers
- **Key Features**: Membership plan tables, class booking calendar, trainer bios, and Stripe payment gateway.
- **Consultant Tip**: Recommend integrating interactive timetables so members can easily check daily class slots.

## 5. E-Commerce Platforms
- **Key Features**: Product catalogs, search filters, shopping carts, secure Stripe/PayPal checkouts, and order confirmation emails.
- **Consultant Tip**: Emphasize page load speed, since every 100ms delay can hurt sales conversion rates by 7%.

## 6. Second Hand Bike Sellers
- **Key Features**: Inventory galleries with filterable tags (brand, size, price), WhatsApp direct negotiating button, and trade-in valuation forms.
- **Consultant Tip**: Suggest integrating a WhatsApp click-to-chat link for every bike listing so buyers can negotiate terms instantly.
