# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - TIA Software Solutions

## Q: Do you provide support after launch?
**A**: Yes! All our plans include continuous maintenance, security updates, and performance monitoring as long as your subscription is active.

## Q: How long does it take to build a website?
**A**: A basic 5-page website typically takes 2–3 weeks. Standard plans take 3–5 weeks. More complex Pro/Premium applications and native mobile apps take 6–10 weeks depending on custom features.

## Q: Can I update content on my site myself?
**A**: Yes. We integrate standard Content Management Systems (CMS) like WordPress, Sanity, or custom markdown panels so you can easily edit text, menus, and images.

## Q: Is hosting and domain registration included?
**A**: Yes. We provide high-speed cloud hosting (via Netlify, Vercel, or AWS) and SSL certificates under our standard monthly subscriptions. We can also assist you with purchasing and linking your custom domain.

## Q: Do you handle Search Engine Optimization (SEO)?
**A**: Yes. All our web plans include core SEO configuration (XML sitemaps, meta descriptions, image optimization, and Google Search Console indexing). Advanced campaigns (PPC ads, keyword ranking growth) are covered under our Digital Marketing plans.
