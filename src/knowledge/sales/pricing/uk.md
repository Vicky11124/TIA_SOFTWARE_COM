# Regional Pricing - United Kingdom (UK)

All rates for UK clients are billed in British Pounds (£ GBP):

- **Basic Plan**: £199.99/mo
  - *Best For*: Small business websites, simple landing pages, brand kits.
  - *Scope*: Under 5 custom pages, responsive design, core SEO, and WhatsApp integration.

- **Standard Plan**: £399.99/mo
  - *Best For*: Growing businesses, interactive websites, service booking sites.
  - *Scope*: 5 to 10 custom pages, responsive layouts, booking calendars, custom styling, performance tuning.

- **Pro Plan**: £649.99/mo
  - *Best For*: E-commerce stores, custom Web Apps, initial Mobile App drafts.
  - *Scope*: Up to 15 pages, online store functionality, payment gateways (Stripe/PayPal), user dashboards.

- **Premium Plan**: £899.99/mo
  - *Best For*: Full mobile app projects, complex web application portals, large-scale digital growth campaigns.
  - *Scope*: Custom native iOS/Android mobile apps, advanced database integrations, full SEM campaigns.
