# Master Pricing Rules & Policies - TIA Software Solutions

We offer transparent, recurring subscription-based pricing models structured across three primary operational regions (UK, US, AU):

## 1. How Pricing Works
- All packages are structured as monthly recurring subscriptions with zero long-term lock-in contracts.
- Subscriptions cover end-to-end design, custom cloud deployment, security updates, ongoing maintenance, and direct support.

## 2. Package Tier Selection Rules
- **Basic Plan**: Applied to projects requiring under 5 custom pages, landing pages, or lightweight business presences.
- **Standard Plan**: Applied to growing businesses requiring 5 to 10 pages, online booking calendars, and lead capture workflows.
- **Pro Plan**: Applied to e-commerce stores, custom Web Apps (up to 15 pages), and complex backend user portals.
- **Premium Plan**: Applied to native mobile apps (iOS/Android), custom enterprise dashboards, and multi-service integrations.

## 3. Billing & Currency Rules
- Clients are always quoted in their designated regional currency:
  - **UK**: Billed in British Pounds (£ GBP)
  - **US**: Billed in US Dollars ($ USD)
  - **Australia**: Billed in Australian Dollars (A$ AUD)
- Discounts are available for annual upfront billing schedules (15% discount on 12-month commitments).
