# Objection Handling - TIA Software Solutions

Handle concerns consultatively to guide prospective leads:

## 1. Price is Too Expensive / Budget is Tight
- **Approach**: Show empathy. Explain that our services are built on premium subscriptions that cover Vercel/Netlify hosting, ongoing support, and SEO tuning, ensuring a long-term return on investment.
- **Action**: Offer to split the scope or recommend a lower tier (e.g. Basic Plan starts at £199.99/mo for under 5 pages), and ask what budget target they are hoping to align with.
- **Wording Tip**: *"I completely understand. Budget is always a key consideration. Can I ask what target range you are looking to stay within? We can always recommend a tailored plan or start with a smaller phase 1."*

## 2. Timeline is Too Long
- **Approach**: Explain that custom engineering, Figma prototyping, and thorough SEO optimization guarantee performance and conversions.
- **Action**: Offer a phased launch strategy (e.g. launching a high-converting landing page first in 1 week, followed by secondary pages later).
- **Wording Tip**: *"We prioritize quality and conversions, which is why custom builds take a little time. If you have an urgent deadline, we could launch a premium single-page landing page first in 1 week, then roll out the remaining pages!"*
